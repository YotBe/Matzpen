## Slide 2 — The Problem

**400,000 Israeli families are managing psychiatric crises alone.**

Three pain points (one image per bullet):

1. **Tracking lives in WhatsApp.** Critical info — sleep, mood, medication — scatters across chats and one person's memory. When a psychiatrist asks "how was the past two weeks?", the family fumbles.
2. **The envelope isn't coordinated.** One or two caregivers carry everything; siblings, partners, friends want to help but don't know how. Burnout is universal.
3. **Emergency paths are opaque.** Involuntary commitment, guardianship, blocking credit, restricting access to lethal means — every family has to figure this out from scratch, in crisis.

**Outcome:** preventable readmissions, financial ruin, family fractures, occasional tragedies.

## Slide 7 — Business model

**B2B2C through the HMOs.**

- HMOs pay ₪15-25/family/month for caregivers of insured psychiatric patients.
- **Why HMOs pay:** average psychiatric readmission costs ₪40,000. Reducing re-admit rate by 5% on 1,000 high-risk families saves ₪2M/year against ~₪240k/year in licensing. **17x ROI.**
- **First pilot:** 100 families × 6 months × ₪20 = ₪120k, with a defined readmission metric.
- **Path to ₪10M ARR:** 1 HMO at full rollout (10k families × ₪20 × 12) or 4 HMOs at 25% each.

**Secondary channels (post-PMF):**
- NGO partnerships (ENOSH / OZMA) — bulk licensing.
- Pharma adherence programs.

## Slide 9 — Moat

Three layers, in order of durability:

1. **Hebrew clinical+legal content library.** LEAP scripts in Hebrew, Israeli court-filing templates (guardianship, exit ban, credit freeze), 12 psychiatric hospital profiles. ~6 months and a clinical advisory board to build. **An English-first competitor can't ship this without doing the entire work over.**
2. **Multi-caregiver "envelope" data structure.** No other dataset has multi-caregiver-per-patient daily-log data, shift histories, or SOS frequency. Two pilots in deep, this becomes a research asset.
3. **HMO relationships.** First kupah pilot creates a 24-month head-start against any new entrant.
